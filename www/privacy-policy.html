Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you use paryx.uk, why it is processed, and the choices and rights you may have. The site is designed to collect as little information about visitors as reasonably possible.

The short version

paryx.uk does not have user accounts, advertising, behavioural profiling, or a contact form. It does not sell or rent personal data. The site mainly displays static content plus public information from external services such as GitHub and Discord-related services.

Who is responsible for the site

paryx.uk is operated by Paryx. For personal data directly controlled through this website or direct email contact, Paryx is responsible for deciding how that information is used.

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to paryx.games@gmail.com.

Information that may be processed

  • Basic technical request data. When you load the site, hosting and network providers may automatically process information such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, request time, and security or diagnostic data needed to deliver and protect the site.
  • Your browser time zone. The homepage reads the time zone reported by your browser so it can show your local time. This value is used in the browser and is not intentionally sent to paryx.uk for storage.
  • Local browser storage. The homepage stores a short-lived cache of public GitHub repository statistics and language data in localStorage. Cache entries are treated as fresh for up to one hour. They may remain on your device until refreshed, overwritten, or cleared by you or your browser. This cache does not contain an account identifier, email address, or information intended to identify you, and it is not used for cross-site tracking.
  • Third-party requests. Some live content, fonts, icons, and activity artwork are loaded from third-party services. When your browser connects to those services, they may receive normal connection information such as your IP address, browser information, and request details.
  • Messages you send. If you contact Paryx by email, the email address, message content, and any other information you choose to include will be processed so the message can be read and answered.

How information is used

Information is used only where reasonably needed to:

  • deliver the website and its features;
  • display public project and live activity information;
  • protect the site, prevent abuse, and investigate technical problems;
  • maintain reliability and performance; and
  • respond to messages or privacy requests you send.

Personal data is not used for advertising, data brokerage, or behavioural profiling.

Lawful basis

Where UK data protection law applies, processing needed to run, secure, maintain, and provide the site is generally carried out on the basis of legitimate interests. Those interests are operating a reliable personal website, protecting it from abuse, and providing the features visitors request.

Information you send by email is processed as necessary to respond to your request and manage the resulting correspondence. If a future feature requires consent, consent will be requested before that processing begins where required by law.

Cookies and similar storage

paryx.uk does not intentionally set first-party advertising or analytics cookies. The homepage currently uses browser localStorage only to cache public GitHub repository data as described above.

Third-party services used by the site operate under their own privacy and storage practices. You can clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Third-party services

The site currently relies on or connects to the following services for specific features:

  • Vercel for website hosting and serverless functionality.
  • GitHub for public repository information and project links.
  • Berrysauce pinned repository API to retrieve publicly visible pinned GitHub repositories.
  • Lanyard for live Discord presence information.
  • Discord for activity images and related public presence assets.
  • Google Fonts for the Inter and Sora typefaces.
  • Simple Icons CDN for certain public icon assets used by live activity cards.
  • Email providers when you choose to contact Paryx by email.

These providers may process technical information independently under their own privacy policies. paryx.uk is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites you choose to visit through links on the site.

Sharing and sale of data

Personal data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers. Information is shared only with service providers where needed to operate the site, respond to communications, comply with a legal obligation, or protect the site and its users.

International processing

Some service providers used by paryx.uk operate internationally. As a result, technical information may be processed outside the United Kingdom. Where applicable, those providers are responsible for using appropriate legal safeguards for their processing and transfers.

How long information is kept

paryx.uk does not maintain a visitor database. Browser-side repository cache entries are treated as current for up to one hour but may remain on your device until they are refreshed or cleared. Hosting, security, and infrastructure logs are retained according to the relevant provider's operational and security settings. Email correspondence is kept only for as long as it is reasonably useful for the conversation, record keeping, security, or legal requirements.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or other information about how your data is used.

You may also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. To make a request, email paryx.games@gmail.com.

If you are in the UK and believe your data protection rights have been breached, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children's privacy

The site is a general personal developer website and is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, forms, advertising, or profiling. If someone contacts Paryx directly, only the information they choose to provide is received and it is handled as described in this policy.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, alteration, loss, or misuse. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so only send information by email that you are comfortable providing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published at this address with the updated date shown near the top.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact paryx.games@gmail.com.
