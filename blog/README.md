# blog.paryx.uk

The blog for [paryx](https://paryx.uk): notes on software, experiments, and lessons learned while building things.

Visit [blog.paryx.uk](https://blog.paryx.uk).

Articles bring together project stories, engineering ideas, and practical discoveries. Each post includes its authors, publication date, topics, and estimated reading time. Full-text search, topic pages, author profiles, and related articles make it easy to explore the archive. Longer articles include a table of contents, while article series offer a reading order and links to the next instalment. Shared articles have a social preview featuring their title and banner.

The design keeps the focus on reading, with a dark background, wide banners, and spacious typography across desktop, tablet, and phone.

Follow new articles through the [RSS feed](https://blog.paryx.uk/rss.xml). Illustrative demo articles are clearly labelled; they are not product announcements.
